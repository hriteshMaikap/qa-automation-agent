Product Brief: Live Incident Response
A live incident is a situation that is still unfolding, such as a fire, security breach, missing-person search, infrastructure failure or storm damage, where several people need to understand what is happening and respond together.
At a fire on a large industrial site, for example, drones provide aerial video, location and telemetry, and people on the ground share their location and phone video. Remote teams need one shared view of who is present, where people and drones are, which feeds are available and whether the information is current. Without it, teams juggle separate calls, messages, maps, video tools and drone dashboards, and it is hard to reconstruct afterwards what happened.
The product is a shared live dashboard where people can join quickly, share information, view connected drones, and later review the sequence of events.
Required capabilities
| Area | Requirement |
| Create and join | Create an incident, generate and copy a secure joining link, join without complicated onboarding, see who has joined, left, connected or disconnected, and end the incident |
| Shared dashboard | One view of incident status and duration, participants and their locations, participant video, connected drones, drone video and telemetry, and data freshness and connection state |
| Drones and live data | Connect one or more drones from the simulation environment through a common simulator interface. Selecting a drone shows its state, telemetry, map location, video, warnings and freshness together. Telemetry fields are not fixed in advance |
| Devices and networks | Works in modern browsers on phones, tablets, laptops and desktops with nothing to install. Stays useful on slow or unstable networks and makes clear whether information is live, delayed, stale, disconnected or unavailable |
| History and review | After the incident ends, review start and end times, who joined or left, participant and drone activity, location and connection changes, shared feeds and simulator events. The closed history is read-only |
Bonus capabilities
Teams may add any of the following. They should be part of the shared product rather than isolated demos.
Chat with mentions and replies
File, image and snapshot sharing
Live video annotations
Shared map drawings, markers and observations
Links between messages, locations, drones and video moments
Multiple video layouts
Alerts and notifications
Participant and drone movement trails
Synchronized replay
Search and filtering within history
Incident summaries and exportable reports
